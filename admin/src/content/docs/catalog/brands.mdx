A brand is a name and a logo that products can point at. Unlike categories,
brands are **entirely optional** — a product with no brand is unbranded, and a
store can have none at all.

Create them on the **Brands** page. There are two fields: **Name** and
**Logo**.

## What a brand actually does

Three things, all in the shopper app:

- Products show their brand on the product details screen.
- The catalog can be **filtered by brand** in the console's product list.
- Brand logos give a produce-and-staples catalog visual texture it otherwise
  lacks.

That's the whole feature. A brand carries no pricing, no stock and no rules.

## When to skip them

Skip brands entirely if you sell:

- **Loose produce** — "Bananas" has no brand, and inventing one is noise.
- **Your own preparations** — a kitchen selling its own food is one brand,
  which is the store.
- **Under ~30 products** — the filter earns nothing at that size.

Use them if you resell packaged goods. A grocery catalog of a few hundred items
becomes much easier to work with when you can filter to one supplier's range.

<Callout type="note">
  Brands are a **flat list** — there's no hierarchy, no parent company, no
  sub-brand. One name, one logo.
</Callout>

## Logos

Brand logos are rendered small and square. Two things that reliably work:

- The official mark on a **white or transparent** background, with padding.
- For house or unbranded lines, a plain monogram tile.

<Callout type="tip">
  Consistency beats fidelity at this size. A set of logos that are all square
  with similar padding looks deliberate; a mix of wide wordmarks and tight
  icons looks broken, even when every individual file is correct.
</Callout>

## Naming

Use the name a shopper would recognise on the packaging. Not the legal entity —
**Amul**, not *Gujarat Cooperative Milk Marketing Federation*. Products
reference brands **by name** on import, so pick the spelling once and keep it.

## Deleting a brand

Products referencing it become unbranded. They keep their name, price, stock,
categories and images — they simply stop showing a brand, and drop out of that
brand's filter. Nothing is lost beyond the association.
