A store isn't visible to shoppers until it's published. Getting there is a
checklist and a review.

## The four states

```text
draft ──submit──► pending ──approve──► published
  ▲                  │                     │
  └──changes─────────┘              ┌──unpublish
  └────────requested────────────────┘
```

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Being built. Visible only to you. |
| **In review** | Submitted. Waiting on us. |
| **Published** | Live for every shopper in the app. |
| **Changes requested** | Sent back, with a reason. Fix it and resubmit. |

Only the server moves a store between these, which is why there's no "publish"
switch you can flip yourself.

## The readiness checklist

Seven checks, shown on your dashboard with a hint for each one that isn't met:

| Check | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Store logo** | Your store's face in discovery. |
| **At least one category** | The storefront's home and browse tabs are built from these. |
| **At least one product** | A store with an empty catalog has nothing to sell. |
| **Payments connected** | Shoppers can't work around not being able to pay. |
| **Store address** | A marketplace has to say who is selling and from where. See [Store profile](/docs/store-setup/store-profile). |
| **Support email** | Shoppers can't work around not being able to reach you. See [Support contact](/docs/store-setup/support-contact). |
| **No banned products** | Alcohol and tobacco can't be sold on the platform. See [Products](/docs/catalog/products). |

## Preview before publishing

The first three checks are the **preview** bar. Clear them and your store
appears in the shopper app's store list — **for you only**, signed in with the
account that owns it. Nobody else can see or open it.

<Callout type="success">
  This is the point of the two tiers. You walk your own half-built store in the
  real app, on a real phone, before a single shopper can find it — and none of
  payments, your support email or your address blocks it, so you can reach
  checkout before wiring money up.
</Callout>

Opening the publish card on your dashboard is what refreshes that preview flag,
so if your store isn't showing up in the app, visit the dashboard first.

## Before you submit

The checklist is a floor, not a standard. Worth doing:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Walk your storefront in the app">
    Home, a category, a product, cart, checkout. Look for empty sections and
    placeholder text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a test order end to end">
    With [test keys](/docs/payments/connect-razorpay). Advance it through the
    console. Cancel one and confirm the refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check every product has a photo">
    Filter the Products page and scan the image column. One missing photo in a
    grid is very visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your delivery areas">
    Empty means everywhere. If you meant to restrict, check the prefixes are
    the right length — see [Delivery and areas](/docs/store-setup/delivery-and-areas).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to live payment keys">
    Test keys can't charge anyone. This is the step people forget.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Submitting

**Submit for review** on the dashboard's publish card. It's enabled once all
six checks pass and your store is in draft or changes-requested.

We check that the store is a real, working shop — a coherent catalog, real
photography, sane prices, working payments. Reviews are quick; you'll get an
outcome rather than silence.

## If changes are requested

You'll get a **reason** on your dashboard saying what to fix. Fix it and submit
again — there's no penalty for resubmitting, and no limit on attempts.

Common reasons: placeholder or generated sample content still in the catalog,
products with no photos, a logo that's illegible at small sizes, or a catalog
that doesn't match what the store says it sells.

<Callout type="warning">
  Don't submit a store full of
  [generated sample data](/docs/catalog/generate-sample-data). It's for
  exploring the console — publishing it means listing products you don't stock,
  at prices you didn't set.
</Callout>

## After you're live

Editing doesn't unpublish. Products, prices, categories, banners, coupons,
delivery, template, language and currency all change on a live store with no
re-review.

A store can also be **unpublished**, which removes it from discovery while
keeping everything intact.
