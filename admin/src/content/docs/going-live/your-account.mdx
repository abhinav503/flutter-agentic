Your account is the sign-in behind every store you own. **Settings → Your
account** shows the three facts about it: your email, your user ID, and when
you joined.

## One account, several stores

An account can own any number of stores, and the **store switcher** at the top
of the sidebar chooses which one you're working on.

<Callout type="warning">
  Every page in the console is scoped to the selected store. If Products looks
  empty or Orders is missing something you know exists, check the switcher
  before checking anything else. It's the most common confusion in the console.
</Callout>

Each store is fully independent: its own catalog, template, language, currency,
payment keys, delivery policy, orders and publication status. Nothing is shared
between them except you.

Create another with **New store** in the same menu.

## Your user ID

The opaque string under your email. You'll rarely need it — it's what you paste
into a support conversation when someone needs to look your account up without
you sending anything sensitive.

<Callout type="note">
  It isn't secret and isn't a credential. Sharing it lets someone identify your
  account; it doesn't let them into it.
</Callout>

## Keeping the account secure

Your account controls real money — your payment keys, your catalog, your
orders.

- **Use a password you don't use anywhere else.** A reused password is the
  realistic way this gets compromised.
- **Don't share the login.** There are no per-person roles yet, so a shared
  password is the only way to give someone access, and it can't be revoked
  without changing it for everyone.
- **Keep payment keys out of chat.** Paste them straight from your provider's
  dashboard into the console. If a key ever lands in a message, regenerate it.

## Shoppers have their own accounts

Worth being clear about: a shopper's account in the app is separate from yours.
You never see their password, and you see only what an order records — their
name, phone and delivery address, snapshotted at placement.

Shoppers can delete their own accounts from the app. That removes their
profile, addresses, cart, favourites, searches and every review they wrote —
but **not their orders**, which remain your sales records.

## Deleting your account

Contact us. It isn't a self-service button in the console, because deleting an
owner account with live stores would strand shoppers mid-order.

If you want to stop trading but keep your data, **unpublish** your store
instead — see [Publish your store](/docs/going-live/publish-your-store). It
disappears from discovery and everything stays intact.
