CordeliaApps is two things that meet in the middle.

<CardGroup>
  <Card title="The admin console" icon="settings">
    Where you build and run your store — catalog, prices, orders, payments,
    notifications. That's the site you're reading this on.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The shopper app" icon="cart">
    A single mobile app that shoppers install once. Every published store
    appears in it, and opening one *restyles the whole app* to that store's
    template.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You don't build an app. You build a **store**, and it appears inside an app
that already exists, is already on the stores, and is already installed on your
shoppers' phones.

## What that means in practice

**You skip app development entirely.** No submission, no review queue, no
version rollout, no waiting a week for Apple to approve a price change. Your
catalog updates the moment you save it.

**Your storefront still looks like yours.** Three templates ship today, each a
complete visual identity — layout, colour, typography, navigation. A shopper
opening your store sees your logo, your palette and your products, not a
generic list.

**Money goes directly to you.** Each store settles into its own Razorpay or
Stripe account. There is no platform wallet holding your revenue and no payout
schedule to wait on — a shopper pays, and it lands in your account.

<Callout type="note">
  Because stores share one app, a shopper who already has CordeliaApps
  installed can open your store the day you publish, with nothing to download.
</Callout>

## The pieces you'll set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="A store">
    Its name, logo, template, language and currency. See
    [Create your first store](/docs/getting-started/create-your-first-store).
  </Step>
  <Step title="A catalog">
    Categories, then brands, then products. Add them by hand, import a CSV, or
    generate a full sample catalog to explore with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A payment account">
    Razorpay for INR, Stripe for EUR, GBP or USD. Your keys, your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A delivery policy">
    What you charge to deliver, and which postal codes you serve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once those four are in place you submit the store for review. Approved stores
become visible to every shopper in the app.

## What CordeliaApps doesn't do

Being straight about the edges is more useful than a feature list:

- **No delivery fleet.** You fulfil your own orders. The console tracks each
  one and issues the shopper a handover code; getting the bag to the door is
  yours.
- **No inventory sync.** Stock is a number on each product that you or an
  import file set. There's no live link to a warehouse system.
- **No currency conversion.** A store charges in one currency. Changing it
  changes what prices are *labelled*, not what they're worth.
