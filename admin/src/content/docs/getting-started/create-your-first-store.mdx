A CordeliaApps store is a real storefront inside the shopper app. You build it
in the admin console, and it appears to shoppers once you publish it. Nothing
you do before that is visible to anyone else, so you can take as long as you
like getting it right.

<Callout type="info">
  You don't need a payment account to start. Payments only gate **publishing**,
  not building — see [Connect Razorpay](/docs/payments/connect-razorpay) when
  you're ready.
</Callout>

## Create your account

Sign up at [cordeliaapps.com](https://cordeliaapps.com) with your email and a
password. Confirm the verification email before continuing — an unverified
account can sign in but can't own a store.

## Set up the store

The console asks for four things the first time you sign in. All four can be
changed afterwards except the store's own identity in existing orders, so pick
deliberately but don't agonise.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name your store">
    This is the name shoppers see in the app and on every order. Use the real
    trading name, not a code name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    The template is your storefront's entire look — layout, colour, typography,
    navigation. Three ship today: **Gravia**, **DailyMart** and **GroFast**.
    Every feature works identically in all three, so this is a visual choice
    only, and you can switch later from Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a language">
    The language your storefront's buttons and labels use. Independent of
    currency — a store can charge in euros and read in English.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a currency">
    What you charge in. This one is worth getting right: it decides which
    payment providers you can use, and changing it after you have orders makes
    your sales history read in two currencies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Razorpay settles in **INR only**. If you pick EUR, GBP or USD, Stripe is your
  payment provider.
</Callout>

## Fill the store out

A brand-new store is empty, and an empty store won't render. Three things make
it previewable:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="A logo" icon="image" href="/docs/store-setup/store-profile">
    Uploaded from Settings → Store profile.
  </Card>
  <Card title="One category" icon="box" href="/docs/catalog/categories">
    Shoppers browse by category, so there has to be at least one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="One product" icon="package" href="/docs/catalog/import-products-csv">
    Add it by hand, import a CSV, or generate a sample catalog.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The fastest way to see something real is the sample-data generator: **Settings →
Sample data → Generate sample data** writes a full grocery catalog — around a
hundred products across 10 categories, with real photography — in one go. It's
meant for exploring the console, not for going live.

## Preview it in the app

Once those three checks pass, your store becomes **preview ready**. It now
appears in the shopper app's store list — but only for you, signed in with the
account that owns it. Nobody else can see or open it.

This is the point of the two-tier check: you walk your own half-built store in
the real app, on a real phone, before a single shopper can find it.

<Accordion title="Why can't I see my store in the app?">
  Preview readiness needs all three: a logo, at least one category, and at
  least one product. The console's readiness checklist on the dashboard shows
  which one is missing. Also confirm you're signed into the app with the same
  email that owns the store — preview visibility is per-account.
</Accordion>
