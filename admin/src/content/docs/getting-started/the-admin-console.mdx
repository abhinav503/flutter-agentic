The console has twelve pages. You'll live in two of them and visit the rest
occasionally. This is a map, not a manual — each page has its own guide.

## The store switcher

Top of the sidebar. One account can own several stores, and **every page in the
console is scoped to the store selected here** — products, orders, coupons, all
of it. If a page looks empty and shouldn't be, check which store is active
before checking anything else.

"New store" in the same menu creates another one.

## Pages you'll set up once

| Page | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings** | Store profile, delivery, payments, sample data, your account. Four cards, and most of your setup happens here. |
| **Categories** | The aisles shoppers browse. A store needs at least one. |
| **Brands** | Optional. Products can be unbranded. |
| **Banners** | The promo carousel on your storefront's home screen. |
| **Stores** | Every store you own, with its publication status. |

## Pages you'll dip into

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Coupons** | Discount codes, with scopes, floors, caps and usage limits. |
| **Reviews** | Moderation. Read what shoppers wrote; delete what breaks your rules. |
| **Notifications** | Compose a push to everyone who follows your store. |

<Callout type="note">
  **Platform notifications** appears only for superadmins — it reaches every
  shopper across every store, not just yours.
</Callout>

## Two things worth knowing early

**Most lists are live.** Orders, products, categories, brands, coupons and
banners stream from the database, so a change made in another tab — or by a
shopper placing an order — appears without a refresh. **Reviews are the
exception**: that page is fetched, and has its own Refresh button.

**Almost nothing is destructive without a confirmation.** Deletes ask first.
The two actions that commit immediately once confirmed are cancelling an order
(it restocks and refunds) and generating sample data (it writes ~150 records).
