**Settings → Store profile** is your storefront's identity. It's what shows in
the shopper app's store list, and it decides what your storefront looks and
reads like once someone opens it.

## The fields

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Store name** | Shown in discovery, on your storefront, and on every order. |
| **Search keywords** | Comma-separated. Discovery search matches these *as well as* your name. |
| **Description** | One short line under your store name in the app. |
| **Store address** | Where you trade from. **Required before you can publish** — a marketplace has to say who is selling and from where. Not where you *deliver*: that's [Delivery and areas](/docs/store-setup/delivery-and-areas). |
| **Logo** | Your store's face in discovery. Required before your store can be previewed. |
| **Template** | Your storefront's entire look. See [Choose a template](/docs/store-setup/choose-a-template). |
| **Language** | The language your storefront's buttons and labels use. |
| **Currency** | What your prices are shown in. |

## Search keywords earn you traffic

The one field people leave blank that they shouldn't. Discovery search matches
your store name *and* these keywords, so they're how a shopper who searched
"vegetables" finds a store called Green Cart.

Write what a shopper would type, not what you'd put on a sign:

```text
grocery, fresh, vegetables, fruits, daily essentials, milk, atta
```

Include local terms and common misspellings you'd realistically be searched
for. There's no penalty for a keyword that never matches.

## Your logo

Required — a store with no logo can't be previewed, let alone published.

<Callout type="tip">
  Use a square image with the subject centred and some breathing room around
  it. It's rendered small and rounded in discovery, so a wide wordmark loses
  its ends and edge-to-edge artwork gets its corners clipped.
</Callout>

## Language and currency are separate axes

This trips people up, so it's worth stating plainly.

**Language** changes the storefront's own words — buttons, labels, section
headings — across every template. Six ship: English, German, French, Spanish,
Italian and Hindi. It does **not** translate your product names; those are
whatever you typed.

**Currency** changes what prices are labelled with, and how they're written.
Four ship: INR, EUR, GBP and USD.

<Callout type="warning">
  Changing currency changes the **display only**. It does not convert your
  prices. Switch a store from INR to EUR and a ₹250 product becomes a €250
  product. Re-price your catalog if you switch.
</Callout>

The two combine freely: a store can charge in euros and read in English. The
resulting formatting is handled for you — a German storefront writes `12,50 €`
where an Indian one writes `₹12.50`, with the decimal comma, the symbol on the
correct side and day-first dates, without you configuring anything.

<Callout type="note">
  Shoppers can switch their *own* language from the storefront's Profile
  screen. Your setting is the default, not a lock.
</Callout>

## What changes when

| Change | When shoppers see it |
| --- | --- |
| Name, description, address, keywords, logo | Next time discovery loads |
| Language, currency | Next time they open your store |
| Template | Next time they open your store |

Nothing here needs a re-review. Editing your profile doesn't unpublish a live
store.
