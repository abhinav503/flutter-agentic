A template is your storefront's whole visual identity: layout, colour,
typography, navigation, iconography, the shape of every card and sheet. Picking
one is the single biggest decision about how your store *feels*.

<Callout type="success">
  **Every feature works identically in all three.** Search, cart, checkout,
  orders, wishlist, reviews, addresses, notifications — all of it, in each
  template. This is a visual choice, never a capability trade-off.
</Callout>

## The three

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Gravia" icon="store">
    Deep green, generous whitespace, rounded cards. Reads calm and premium —
    suits curated or specialty catalogs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DailyMart" icon="cart">
    Bright green, denser grids, bordered strips. Reads busy and everyday —
    suits a wide general catalog.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GroFast" icon="truck">
    Soft green with gradient accents, domed sheets, a lifted nav bar. Reads
    fast and playful — suits quick-commerce.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to pick

Ignore the colour first — all three are green, because grocery is. Pick on
**density**, which is the difference a shopper actually feels:

- A catalog of **under ~50 products** looks sparse in a dense grid. Gravia's
  larger cards carry it better.
- A catalog of **hundreds** wants rows on screen. DailyMart and GroFast fit
  more above the fold.
- If speed is your pitch — minutes, not days — GroFast's motion and shape
  language says so before any copy does.

<Callout type="tip">
  You don't have to decide from a description. Set one, open your store in the
  app, and switch. Nothing you've built is tied to a template.
</Callout>

## Switching

Change it in **Settings → Store profile → Template** and save. The new look
applies the next time a shopper opens your store.

Nothing else moves. Your catalog, categories, brands, banners, coupons, orders,
reviews and payment configuration are stored independently of any template —
switching restyles the storefront and touches nothing underneath. A live store
does not need re-approval to change template.

<Accordion title="Can I customise a template's colours?">
  Not today. Each template is a fixed design sampled from a complete UI kit,
  and its palette, type scale and shapes are tuned as one system — a
  half-recoloured template looks worse than either of the originals. Your
  identity comes through your logo, photography and copy.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can different stores I own use different templates?">
  Yes. Template is per-store, so a specialty shop can run Gravia while your
  general store runs DailyMart, under one account.
</Accordion>
