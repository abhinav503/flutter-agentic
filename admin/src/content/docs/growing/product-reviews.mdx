Any signed-in shopper can rate a product 1–5 with optional text. Ratings appear
on product cards throughout your storefront, which makes them one of the few
things on your store you don't fully control.

Read and moderate them on the **Reviews** page.

## The rules

- **One review per shopper per product.** Posting again edits the existing one
  rather than adding a second.
- **No purchase required.** Anyone signed in can review anything in your store.
- **Purchase is a badge, not a gate.** A shopper who has a *delivered* order
  containing that product gets a **verified purchase** mark on their review.

<Callout type="note">
  The badge is derived server-side from delivered orders — it isn't something
  a shopper claims or you assign. It's how a reader tells a real customer's
  review from a passer-by's.
</Callout>

## How ratings reach product cards

Every product carries a rolled-up **average**, **review count** and a
distribution across the five star levels. These move in the same transaction as
every review write, so a product's rating and its reviews can never drift
apart.

They aren't editable. There is no field to set a product's rating — it's a
consequence of reviews, and the only way to change it is for the reviews to
change.

## Order ratings are different

Two separate things, easy to confuse:

| | Rates | Who can | Public |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Product review** | A product | Any signed-in shopper | Yes — on the storefront |
| **Order rating** | A delivery | Only that order's shopper, once delivered | No — console only |

An order rating is feedback on how the delivery went. It shows on the order in
your console and nowhere else. See
[Managing orders](/docs/orders/managing-orders).

## Moderation

The Reviews page lists every review across your store, with the product name,
the rating, the text, the reviewer and whether they're a verified purchaser.

You have exactly one action: **delete**. There's no edit, no reply, no hiding.
Deleting removes the review and corrects the product's aggregates in the same
transaction, so the average is right immediately.

<Callout type="warning">
  Deleting a review because it's negative is the wrong use of this. A store
  whose worst review is four stars reads as filtered, and shoppers discount the
  whole set. Delete abuse, spam and content about the wrong product — not
  criticism.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
  Unlike the rest of the console, this page **isn't live** — it's fetched, with
  its own **Refresh** button. A review posted while you're looking at it
  appears on the next load.
</Callout>

## Reading them usefully

- **A one-star with detail is worth more than ten five-stars.** It's a bug
  report about your catalog: a wrong photo, a wrong size, a stale price.
- **Watch for repeats.** The same complaint on three products in a category is
  a sourcing or listing problem, not three unhappy shoppers.
- **Unverified reviews on a product nobody bought** are worth a look. That's
  the pattern spam takes.
